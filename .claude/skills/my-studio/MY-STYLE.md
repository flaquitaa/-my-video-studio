# My Style

My look: captions, colours, pacing, and anything I never want.

Learned from my reference video (85 s "5 steps" tutorial, 1080x1920, 30 fps), 2026-11-09. I said: "I like all." Learn the style, never copy the video.

## Default codes

H10 + X1 · C1 (with C7 serif keyword) · L4 · P7 / P8 · M3 · G5

Plus, when the video fits: V20 step titles, L9 / V23 before-after, M7 colour circle wipe, B6 Dock click. Suggest the right set per video and confirm with me; don't force every device into every video.

## Look

- **Hook (H10 + X1):** two-weight title up top from frame 1: a big butter-gold serif italic line over small, widely tracked white caps (like "My Claude Edits / SYSTEM"). My past reels float around me as rounded phone cards. A yellow sticker label with a black outline and bold black text under the title ("5 Steps → No Course"). Title leaves after about 3 s.
- **Captions (C1 + C7 keyword):** 2 to 4 words per line, one line. White bold sans, sentence case, soft shadow. Sit low, under the chin, never on the face. One keyword per line is swapped for a butter-gold serif italic (nouns and the point word: "Claude", "raw", "video", "Storyboard", "Love").
- **Section titles:** "Step N:" in white bold sans + the step name in gold serif italic, small twinkling sparkles above, top of frame.
- **Layout (L4):** my normal full shot most of the time. Graphics float in the clear space above my head as clean white rounded UI cards (G4 style: soft shadow, small label like "SCREENSHOT THIS").
- **Proof (P7 / P8):** real screen recordings with the cursor, or a phone-filmed monitor with my finger pointing. Captions keep running over them.
- **Before / after (L9 / V23):** "Raw Clip" over "Option A · B · C" cards on a black canvas, same serif-italic label style.
- **Transitions (M7):** a flat butter-yellow full frame with a circle that zooms into the next shot (e.g. into the Mac Dock). Otherwise hard cuts.
- **Colour (G5):** warm, golden, cosy grade. Accent colour is butter gold/yellow; text is white. Keep the warm natural light in my room.
- **Fonts:** gold accents in a thin serif italic (Instrument Serif italic in the style guide fonts); body captions in a bold sans (Inter / Inter Tight).

## Pacing

- **M3:** a new visual (card, screen, title, cutaway) about every 3 seconds. Reference measured 26 big visual changes in 85 s (one every ~3.3 s).
- Structure tutorials as numbered steps, each with its own section title.

## Sound

- Reference measured -19.4 LUFS. Keep the house rule: mix to about -14 LUFS, effects under my voice.
